## 三、攻擊路線

### 路線 A: CY₃ 幾何強制

N_gen = 3 來自 CY₃(36,98) 嘅 Hodge 數結構:
- h²¹−h¹¹ = 62 = 44+18
- 62 分解: 44 = N_d (K_Chain 維度), 18 = L₆ (Lucas 數)
- N_gen = h²¹−h¹¹−N_d/L₆ = 18/6 = 3

但 L₆=18 點解除 6 得 3? 6 係 B+2? 6 = L₄? 6 = F₅?
- 6 = L₄ (Lucas: 2,1,3,4,7,11,18,...)
- 6 = F₅ (Fibonacci: 1,1,2,3,5,8,...)
- 6 = N_d/L₄? 44/4=11. No.
- 6 = B+2 = 4+2. Yes!

所以: N_gen = (h²¹−h¹¹−N_d)/L₆ = 18/6 = 3
     = L₆/(B+2) = 18/6 = 3

### 路線 B: SU(12) 代數強制

66 = Λ²(12) = (6,6)⊕(10,3̄)
- 3̄ = 3 = N_gen (世代)
- 3̄ appears in the decomposition of 66 under SU(6)×SU(6) or SU(10)×SU(3)
- 3 = N_gen is the SU(3) factor in the SU(10)×SU(3) decomposition

### 路線 C: K_Tree 結構強制

K_Tree = 33 = 3×11 = N_gen × L₅
- 33 = 3×11: 3 世代 × 11 維
- 11 = L₅ = rank of SU(12)
- 3 = N_gen 係 K_Tree 嘅世代因子
