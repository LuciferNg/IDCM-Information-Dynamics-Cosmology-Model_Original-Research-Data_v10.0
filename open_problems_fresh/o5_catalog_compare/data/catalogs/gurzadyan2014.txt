                                                                                                        ABSTRACT

                                                   The structure of the cold spot, of a non-Gaussian anomaly in the cosmic microwave background (CMB) sky first detected
                                                   by Vielva et al. is studied using the data by Planck satellite. The obtained map of the degree of stochasticity (K-map)
                                                   of CMB for the cold spot, reveals, most clearly in 100 GHz band, a shell-type structure with a center coinciding with
                                                   the minima of the temperature distribution. The shell structure is non-Gaussian at a 4σ confidence level. Such behavior
                                                   of the K-map supports the void nature of the cold spot. The applied method can be used for tracing voids that have
                                                   no signatures in redshift surveys.
                                                   Key words. cosmic background radiation
