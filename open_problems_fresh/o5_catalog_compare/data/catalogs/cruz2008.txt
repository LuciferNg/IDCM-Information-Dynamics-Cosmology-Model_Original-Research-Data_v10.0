                                                                             ABSTRACT

                                                                                  The non-Gaussian cold spot found in the WMAP data has created controversy
                                                                             about its origin. Here we calculate the Bayesian posterior probability ratios for three
                                                                             different models that could explain the cold spot. A recent work claimed that the
                                                                             Spot could be caused by a cosmic texture, while other papers suggest that it could
                                                                             be due to the gravitational effect produced by an anomalously large void. Also the
                                                                             Sunyaev-Zeldovich effect caused by a cluster is taken into account as a possible origin.
                                                                             We perform a template fitting on a 20◦ radius patch centered at Galactic coordi-
                                                                             nates (b = −57◦ , l = 209◦ ) and calculate the posterior probability ratios for the void
                                                                             and Sunyaev-Zeldovich models, comparing the results to those obtained with texture.
                                                                             Taking realistic priors for the parameters, the texture interpretation is favored, while
                                                                             the void and Sunyaev-Zeldovich hypotheses are discarded. The temperature decre-
                                                                             ment produced by voids or clusters is negligible considering realistic values for the
                                                                             parameters.
                                                                             Key words: methods: data analysis - cosmic microwave background
