$$\text{Tr}(K) = \sum_{n=1}^{44} \sum_{m=0}^{M} \gamma_n \mu_m = \left(\sum_{n=1}^{44} \gamma_n\right) \left(\sum_{m=0}^{M} \mu_m\right)$$

$$\chi = 4M + 2 \quad \rightarrow \quad M = \frac{\chi - 2}{4} = \frac{134 - 2}{4} = 33$$

**證明：**

**步驟 1：** 由 K_Chain 證明，N_d = 44 = 4 × 11（唯一）。
